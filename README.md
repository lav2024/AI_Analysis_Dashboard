# AI Influence on Student Personality and Career Behaviour

## 📌 Project Overview

**AI Influence on Student Personality and Career Behaviour** is an AI usage analysis dashboard designed to explore how students' interactions with Artificial Intelligence (AI) may relate to their learning habits, creativity, emotional well-being, extracurricular involvement, and career decisions.

AI tools have become an important part of modern education. When used effectively, they can support learning, improve productivity, and provide career guidance. However, inappropriate use or excessive dependence on AI may reduce independent problem-solving, critical thinking, and students' confidence in their own abilities.

This project uses data visualization to examine these patterns and help users understand the potential positive and negative influences of AI on students.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the relationship between AI usage and students' learning-related behaviour.
- Explore how AI usage may relate to creativity and independent thinking.
- Understand students' emotional perceptions and dependence on AI.
- Examine the relationship between extracurricular involvement and career counselling trends.
- Present data-driven insights through an interactive dashboard.
- Encourage responsible and balanced AI usage among students.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. AI Influence Analysis
Explores students' perceptions of AI and its possible influence on their personality, creativity, and emotional behaviour.

### 2. Creativity and Emotional Dependence
Visualizes the relationship between creativity-related indicators and emotional influence scores to identify patterns in the observed data.

### 3. Career Counselling Analysis
Examines career counselling trends alongside extracurricular involvement levels.

### 4. Student Perception Dashboard
Uses visual representations to explore peer perceptions of AI usage and student creativity.

### 5. Interactive Data Visualization
Includes charts, KPI cards, bar charts, line charts, and pie charts to make the analysis easier to understand.

### 6. Data-Driven Insights
Helps identify patterns that can support discussions about effective AI usage, student independence, and responsible technology adoption.

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes the following visualizations:

- **Impact of Extracurricular Activities on Student Creativity** – explores patterns between extracurricular involvement and creativity-related indicators.
- **Career Counselling Trends Across Extracurricular Involvement Levels** – compares career counselling trends across involvement levels.
- **KPI Overview** – displays a key numerical indicator from the analyzed data.
- **Peer Perception of AI Usage and Student Creativity Insights** – presents perception-related data through a treemap.
- **Relationship Between Creativity and Emotional Dependence** – compares creativity-related measures with emotional influence indicators.
- **AI Emotional Influence and Perception Analysis** – summarizes positive, negative, and neutral perceptions using a pie chart.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **Microsoft Power BI** – dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Data Analysis** – exploring relationships and trends in the dataset.
- **Charts and KPIs** – presenting findings in an accessible visual format.

## 💡 Key Insights and Motivation

The project is motivated by the observation that the impact of AI depends partly on how students use it.

- **Responsible usage:** AI can assist with understanding concepts, exploring ideas, and improving productivity.
- **Overdependence:** Relying on AI for every task may limit opportunities to practice independent thinking and problem-solving.
- **Learning and creativity:** Students should use AI to support their learning rather than replace their own effort.
- **Emotional influence:** Understanding students' perceptions of AI can help encourage balanced and mindful use.
- **Career development:** AI can be a useful source of guidance, but students should also develop their own judgement and decision-making skills.

These points represent the motivation behind the analysis, not conclusions that apply to every student.

## 🌍 Project Impact

This dashboard aims to promote awareness of responsible AI adoption in education. It can help students, educators, and researchers explore usage patterns and discuss ways to balance AI-assisted learning with independent thinking, creativity, and personal development.

## ⚠️ Limitations

- The dashboard presents patterns and relationships in the available data.
- Observed associations do not establish that AI usage directly causes changes in students' knowledge, creativity, or personality.
- The results depend on the quality, scope, and representativeness of the dataset.
- The dashboard is intended for exploratory analysis and awareness, not for diagnosing students or making definitive judgments about their abilities.

## 🚀 Future Enhancements

- Integrate additional student learning and AI usage data.
- Compare learning outcomes across different AI usage patterns.
- Add filters for student groups, usage frequency, and learning activities where the data supports them.
- Develop a responsible AI usage awareness score using clearly defined and validated criteria.
- Add actionable recommendations for students and educators.

## 👩‍💻 Conclusion

**AI is a tool, and its impact depends on how it is used.**

This project explores the relationship between AI usage and different aspects of student development through data visualization. Its goal is to encourage students to use AI as a learning companion while continuing to build their own knowledge, creativity, critical thinking, and decision-making abilities.
